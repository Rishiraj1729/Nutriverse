Why we exist

INDIA HAD THE INGREDIENT.
SNACKING DIDN’T.

Nutriverse was built on a simple observation. India has always had great ingredients, but modern snacking was stuck between fried junk and boring health food. We wanted something better.

So we roast makhana — fox nuts handpicked from Bihar — in olive oil. Never fried. No palm oil. Light enough for every day. Crunchy enough to actually crave.

“We don’t make health food that pretends to be a snack. We make snacks that happen to be better.”

Anurag Singh & Aditya Singh · Co-founders
