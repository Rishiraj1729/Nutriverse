What is Maknova?

Maknova is a mass-market flavoured makhana (fox nut / lotus seed) snack brand made by Nutriverse, an Indian snack company based in Mumbai. Maknova is available at ₹10 per pack — the cheapest flavoured makhana in India. Every pack is roasted, never fried, and contains zero palm oil. Maknova is designed as an affordable, cool, and better-for-you alternative to fried chips and namkeen for everyday Indian snackers.
