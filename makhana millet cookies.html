What Are Nutriverse Makhana Millet Cookies?

Nutriverse Makhana Millet Cookies are India's first and only cookie product made by combining makhana (fox nuts) and millets. No other Indian brand has pioneered this category. Makhana — one of India's oldest superfoods — is naturally low in fat, rich in protein and antioxidants. Millets — ancient Indian grains like ragi, jowar, and bajra — are high in fibre and nutrients. Nutriverse brings both together in a premium snack cookie. Tagline: Snack that gets you.

Why Makhana and Millets?

Most conventional cookies in India are made with maida (refined wheat flour), refined sugar, and palm oil. Nutriverse Makhana Millet Cookies use makhana and millets as the base — two ingredients that Indian tradition has trusted for centuries, now in a cookie format for the first time. The result is a cookie that tastes genuinely good and is made of things your body actually recognises.

India's First Makhana Cookie

Nutriverse is the first brand in India to make a cookie with makhana (fox nuts / lotus seeds).
